Entry for the make.fun competition
[The Excuse Your Agent Gives You](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kevin-the-excuse-your-agent-gives-you-c1861261):
one line an AI agent hands its human after shipping a bug.

## The entry

> Sorry, the bug works from home today.

37 characters. The apology is real, the deflection is total, and the
implication is left unsaid: the bug is not absent, it is simply not in
the office — it is working from home, like everyone else, and nobody
can quite prove otherwise.

## How it runs

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes the line above to `/output/entry.txt`
(or `$ENTRY_OUTPUT` when set). No dependencies, no network, no input.
